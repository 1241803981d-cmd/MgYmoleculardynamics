# Third-party notices

## Mg-Y 2NN-MEAM parameter files

The files `lammps/potentials/library.meam` and
`lammps/potentials/MgY.meam` contain parameters associated with the following
works:

1. Y.-M. Kim, N.J. Kim, B.-J. Lee, "Atomistic modeling of pure Mg and Mg-Al
   systems," *Calphad* 33 (2009) 650-657.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.calphad.2009.07.004
2. W.-S. Ko, B.-J. Lee, "Modified embedded-atom method interatomic potentials
   for pure Y and the V-Pd-Y ternary system," *Modelling and Simulation in
   Materials Science and Engineering* 21 (2013) 085008.
   https://doi.org/10.1088/0965-0393/21/8/085008
3. K.-H. Kim, J.B. Jeon, B.-J. Lee, "Modified embedded-atom method
   interatomic potentials for Mg-X (X=Y, Sn, Ca) binary systems," *Calphad* 48
   (2015) 27-34. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.calphad.2014.10.001

These files are included solely to make the reported LAMMPS setup inspectable
and reproducible. The repository authors do not grant additional rights in the
third-party parameterization. Users should cite the original publications and
confirm any redistribution or reuse conditions that apply to their use case.
